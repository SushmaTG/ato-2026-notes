# ATO – Open Source in AI Notes

Nov 9, 2026 · @Sushma

Open source is now the biggest supply-chain risk in AI, and open models are only about 4 months behind the frontier.

## Main points from the slides

- **Supply chain:** open source is the biggest supply-chain risk in AI.
- **Build in public:** it creates the chance for people to discover your work.
- **Agents choose now:** agents are making the decisions, so you have to earn their choice.
- **Process matters:** how you got there matters as much as your code now.
- **Open models are close:** the best open-weight model is only about 4 months behind the best closed-weight one (Chatbot Arena, October 2026). The slide's chart runs from Claude 1 and Vicuna-33B in 2023 to models such as DeepSeek V4-Pro, Kimi K3 and MiMo-V2.6-Pro in 2026, with the gap narrowing.
